Delete Account

To delete your ADAM account, follow the steps below. Make sure you are signed in to the app.

Open ADAM App
Steps
1 Open the ADAM app and sign in
2 Tap the menu (☰) then tap "Sign out"
3 In the dialog that appears, tap "Delete account"
4 Confirm the account deletion

When you delete your account, all personal data (name, email) will be permanently removed. Processing the request may take up to 3 business days.
